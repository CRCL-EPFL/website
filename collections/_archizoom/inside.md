The exhibition brings together projects addressing automation, labour, and sustainability in construction. It situates contemporary robotics within a longer history of mechanisation, industrial production, and automation.

Through live robotic demonstrations, built prototypes, films, and documentation, the works range from autonomous assembly to collaborative processes in which humans and machines build, adapt, and respond to uncertain conditions.

Rather than imagining robots simply as substitutes for workers, the exhibition asks how machines might support human capacities and contribute to more adaptive and resource-conscious ways of building.
