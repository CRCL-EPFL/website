title: What Roles Should Robots Play in Construction?
---
Robots have long promised to relieve workers from dangerous and physically demanding tasks while making building faster and more efficient. Yet construction remains largely manual, environmentally destructive, and deeply dependent on human skill and judgement.

**Do Robots Dream of Construction Sites?** examines this contradiction. Through robotic demonstrations, prototypes, films, and research developed at EPFL, the exhibition explores alternatives to automation as replacement: processes in which humans and machines assemble, adapt, experiment, and build together.
